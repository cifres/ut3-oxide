\section{Goals \& Non-goals}

Here we discuss the project's architecture, driving algorithms
such as the AI ones, Elo, matchmaking, and multiplayer protocols.


As briefly mentioned in the introduction, the following are the goals of this project.
Furthermore, to lead the Ultimate Tic Tac Toe space, the website must meet the following goals, and be executed
to the highest quality possible. This ensures that website from its early days is seen as reputable, reliable, and trustwothy 
given the self-evident quality and standard. Here are some user experience (UX) enhancing goals. 

\label{sec:goals}

\subsection{Goals}
\begin{itemize}
   \item \textbf{Online/Local Multiplayer}: this encompasses the follwoing: 
    \begin{itemize}
        \item Open lobbies and spectating them.
        \item Private, link-only invites.
        \item \textbf{Correspondence mode} where a match is played over several days.
    \end{itemize}
   
   \item \textbf{AI}: Medium, and Hard difficulty achieved by the current
    $\alpha$-$\beta$ pruning Minimax algorithm, 
    and the Monte Carlo Treesearch (MCTS) algorithm, respectively.
    \item \textbf{Additional features}: computer analysis, optional game evaluation bar versus AI, match history, elo
    \item \textbf{Meta-level features}: Matchmaking, Match History
\end{itemize}

\subsubsection {Future, long-term goals}

\begin{itemize}
    \item Tutorial
    \item Teaching tactics
\end{itemize}

\subsection {Non-goals}
\begin{itemize}
    \item Social capabilities — instead a Discord server will be created to foster and grow the community
\end{itemize}
